## Page sequence (chicago #3 / PAGANI MIAMI)
1. **Hero.** Utopia at night in the Mantova piazza; the Utopia script logo in a small plate; "Inspired, at the highest level, by beauty and scientific research."; EXPLORE.
2. **"2025 Model Lineup / HYPERCARS".** A cutout on a dark studio ground. The model logotype sits top-right, and the neighbouring cars peek in, blurred, at both edges. A tab rail (HUAYRA R EVO ROADSTER · UTOPIA · UTOPIA ROADSTER · HUAYRA BC), then a paragraph and "Learn More". Four states.
3. **"GRANDI COMPLICAZIONI".** A staggered 2×2 of portrait cards (Epitome, Imola Roadster, Codalunga, Zonda HP Barchetta) over a sunset-track background. Each card holds a model script logo and specs.
4. **History.** Horacio Pagani's signature, "The boy who wanted to create the most beautiful car in the world.", and a sepia portrait.
5. **Service.** "Meticulous craftsmanship and precision service, worldwide." on a centred plate over two Paganis on a road.
6. **Footer.** Dealer address and CTA, with a silhouette of the car at the bottom.

## Keep (what works)
- A dark, cinematic ground of blue-grey-violet night, and a single hero scene.
- Model logotypes as the identity of each model.
- The line-up as **one stage with a switcher**, with neighbours peeking in at the edges. This is the strongest idea in the file and a natural candidate for the motion signature.
- Grandi Complicazioni as a separate chapter for the one-offs and few-offs. It is a distinct register: collector pieces, not the range.
- Horacio's story as the brand chapter.

## Do better (findings)
- **Copy.** It is placeholder or AI text in Title Case ("Utopi Pagani Is A Dreamy Fusion… Cluttercore… AI-Generated Fantasy"). Pagani explicitly rejected Title Case and the "they" voice. All copy is to be rewritten from sources (CP3: a prior concept is not a source).
- **Chicago facts on the Miami page.** The address, phone, "70,000 square foot facility in Lake Bluff" and "Copyright Pagani of Chicago" all have to go. Every Miami fact must come from the ledger.
- **Logos.** The early frames use the R EVO script without "Huayra", which Pagani called wrong. The Miami lockup exists only as a raster. Logos are now SVG only.
- **Spec copy.** All four Grandi Complicazioni cards carry the same Epitome spec text.
- **Composition.** Large empty dark bands between chapters (frame 1:2). The service plate is a boxed card over the photo. Type is small: 12–14 px labels at 1920.
- **Motion.** Undeclared. The line-up switcher, the peek-in neighbours and the hero are where motion should live. See the MOTION READ (to be written).
