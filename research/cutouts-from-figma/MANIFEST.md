**Notes**
- **Resolution.** R Evo (1152 px) and Utopia Roadster (1122 px) are too small to be the hero at full width. They are fine as tiles. For larger use we need a higher-resolution source, or a fal upscale; an upscaled file gets the `gen-` prefix and a sidecar (GI6).
- **Origin.** These are official Pagani press imagery that was cut out earlier. Cutting out changes only the background, so they are not synthetic depictions (GI3 is OK). Usage rights still need confirming with Pagani.
- **Figma copy is not reusable.** The model descriptions in Lake Forest are placeholder or AI text: "Utopi Pagani Is A Dreamy Fusion… Cluttercore… AI-Generated Fantasy". They are in the Title Case Pagani objected to, and Chicago facts (address, phone, "70,000 square foot facility in Lake Bluff") sit in the Miami page. Every string has to be rewritten from sources.
- **The Revo logo.** The older Lake Forest frames use the "R EVO Roadster" script **without "Huayra"**, which Pagani flagged as wrong. The newer PRESTIGE-file frames use the correct "Huayra R EVO Roadster".
